# 🎬 SCENE SCENARIOS — 12 Webpage Backdrop Scenes

*Creative brief for the art team. Each scene = a location backdrop with 2–3 featured guild characters caught mid-moment. Generate the cast (One Piece paper-doll style) composited into each backdrop.*

**Casting source:** characters drawn from the **top ~30 by guild rank** (season M+ score, `board_state.json`). Each character headlines **only one** scene — no reuse — so the spotlight spreads across the roster. Brewzleeh's signature **gambling** bit is reserved for **Undermine**.

**Read each entry as:** Scene → *vibe* → **featured characters** → the one-line mini-story the art should capture (an action/moment, not a line-up).

---

## 1. 🌸 Silvermoon City
*Vibe: opulent sin'dorei arcane spires, red-gold banners, eternal autumn.*
**Featured:** Rakdisc · Enyò · Ohnomyais
**Mini-story:** Rakdisc (Nightborne disc priest) drags the crew through the Court of the Sun on a transmog run, holding a robe up to the light while Enyò and Ohnomyais fake enthusiasm for the fourth identical shade of crimson.

## 2. 🦁 Stormwind City
*Vibe: Alliance grandeur, blue-and-gold trumpets, the canals and cathedral.*
**Featured:** Tommybravoo · Jovala · Kirrá
**Mini-story:** Tommybravoo strikes a hero pose on the Trade District bridge for the guild recruitment poster while Jovala tries to hold the camera steady and Kirrá "accidentally" ninjas the fountain coins behind them.

## 3. 🪓 Orgrimmar
*Vibe: brutal red-iron Horde capital, spike towers, Valley of Strength drums.*
**Featured:** Rexxhavocc · Boondocka · Ellebasi
**Mini-story:** Rexxhavocc squares up in the Valley of Honor brawler's ring, Boondocka taking side-bets on the fight while Ellebasi quietly reads the training dummy's "damage meter" and declares themselves the real winner.

## 4. 🔥 The Dark Portal
*Vibe: seething green fel gateway, shattered Blasted Lands stone, ember sky.*
**Featured:** Hellful · Impkiller · Martylol
**Mini-story:** Impkiller boots a stray imp back through the swirling portal like a field goal, Hellful cheering the distance while Martylol films it for the memes channel instead of watching the actual demon horde forming behind them.

## 5. 🔮 Dalaran
*Vibe: floating violet mage-city, snowy streets, arcane clocktower glow.*
**Featured:** Rhansîck · Buchalter · Kelova
**Mini-story:** Buchalter does frantic arcane math on a floating chalkboard trying to prove the guild bank is short, while Rhansîck and Kelova toss illegal fireballs off the underbelly of the city just to watch them fall.

## 6. 💀 Icecrown Citadel
*Vibe: cold cobalt necropolis, saronite spires, Lich King's frozen throne above.*
**Featured:** Shadoxii · Shadoxi · Aiime
**Mini-story:** The Shadox twins pose for a "menacing" photo on the frozen throne steps — one throwing up rock-horns, the other slipping on the ice — as Aiime solemnly plants a guild banner in a snowdrift that immediately falls over.

## 7. 🌲 Elwynn Forest
*Vibe: golden-hour starter zone, autumn oaks, Northshire chapel and lazy streams.*
**Featured:** Healyeah · Arfas
**Mini-story:** Healyeah (Dracthyr) belly-flops off the Crystal Lake bridge to fish while Arfas naps against a haystack, both level-1'ing it up on a nostalgia day instead of pushing keys.

## 8. 🌳 Mount Hyjal / World Tree
*Vibe: colossal Nordrassil under starlight, cascading waterfalls, verdant green.*
**Featured:** Healmates · Phyrthepali · Tinyx
**Mini-story:** At the World Tree's roots, Phyrthepali (top healer) tries to lead a solemn "healer appreciation" ritual while Healmates gets openly bullied about a mid-fight death and Tinyx spams heart emotes to keep the peace.

## 9. ⚒️ Ironforge
*Vibe: molten dwarven forge-halls, brass and stone, the Great Forge roaring.*
**Featured:** Kegsmashroll · Flogeron · Yur
**Mini-story:** Kegsmashroll wins a Stonehearth stein-slamming contest by a mile, Flogeron passed out under the table, Yur riding a runaway forge-cart of ale kegs across the Great Forge platform.

## 10. ⏳ Caverns of Time / Tanaris
*Vibe: bronze-lit timeways swirling over golden desert dunes, hourglass sands.*
**Featured:** Rakell · Onessarain · Tommybravox
**Mini-story:** Rakell argues with a Bronze Dragonflight keeper about "just one" reset to un-pull the last wipe, while Onessarain and Tommybravox loot the same treasure chest across three different timelines at once.

## 11. 🦖 Un'Goro Crater
*Vibe: prehistoric jungle basin, steaming volcano, ferns and roaming dinosaurs.*
**Featured:** Amrevenge · Floofwall
**Mini-story:** Amrevenge (BM hunter, +20 key record-holder) tames a snorting devilsaur mid-charge while Floofwall the pandaren brewmaster calmly brews tea on its back, betting it'll be the guild's best new tank.

## 12. 💰 Undermine (Goblin Capital)
*Vibe: neon goblin boomtown, cash-green signage, oil, smog and slot machines.*
**Featured:** Brewzleeh · Maillo
**Mini-story:** **Brewzleeh runs a rigged three-card-monte / coin-flip table under the Undermine neon**, raking in the guild's gold as Maillo — already deep in gambling debt — shoves one more "guaranteed winner" bet across the crate.
