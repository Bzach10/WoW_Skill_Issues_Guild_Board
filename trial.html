How it works

Nobody has to type anything in by hand

STEP 1

The data comes in

Every week the board pulls real numbers from Raider.io and Warcraft Logs — Mythic+ scores, timed keys, parses, attendance. The guild roster is fetched automatically.

STEP 2

The cast is drawn

Each member's real character — race, class, spec, transmog — is turned into original art in the style you're looking at. New art drops in and the board picks it up on the next build.

STEP 3

The board assembles

Art and data are combined into the page: the crew, the ladder, the roast, the voyage map. Anything missing degrades quietly instead of breaking the page.

STEP 4

It posts itself

Once you're happy, the weekly board posts to Discord and updates the website automatically every Tuesday. Not switched on for this trial.

What changed in this draft

Fixes and additions since the first look

Fixed

  • Character links opened blank pages. Every player link was built from the guild's realm, but most of the roster plays on other realms — 70% of 134 members, across 37 realms. Rakdisc is on Proudmoore, not the guild realm. Links now use each player's real realm, and anyone we can't confirm gets no link rather than a broken one.
  • The Raid / Mythic+ view didn't filter. Those buttons were scroll anchors that looked like tabs. They now actually filter the board, and remember your choice.
  • Headgear was invisible. The art layers put faces above hats, so Rakdisc's mitre was hidden. Fixed in the layer spec — you can see the mitre in her scene above.

Added

  • A real page for every member, with their art, score, guild rank, streak and records.
  • An animated crew deck where the cast stand together and react when you hover a role.
  • Three themes, switchable at the top right — try them.

Still coming

  • Scene art for the rest of the guild — 3 drawn, 130 still in the queue.
  • The voyage map wired to the live season dungeon list.
  • Victory and defeat poses driven by weekly results.
